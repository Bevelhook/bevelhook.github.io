Provenance

Off-grid, from stump to table

Near Viola, where Tenny Creek begins, a winter storm brought down a black cherry tree. We didn't let it rot or burn. We milled it on our land with a small, fuel-efficient sawmill. Our land has no power lines.

The slabs air-dried for three years under cover, then went into a kiln we built ourselves and fire with wood. Every piece is shaped, joined and finished by hand, with a signed certificate telling the tree's story.
